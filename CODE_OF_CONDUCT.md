# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

## 我们的承诺 / Our Pledge

为了营造一个开放、友好、包容的环境，我们作为贡献者和维护者承诺：

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to:

- 尊重所有参与者，无论其年龄、体型、残疾、族裔、性别认同和表达、经验水平、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教信仰或性取向
- 使用友好、包容的语言
- 尊重不同的观点和经验
- 优雅地接受建设性批评
- 关注对社区最有利的事情
- 对其他社区成员表示同理心

- Make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy towards other community members

## 我们的标准 / Our Standards

### 积极行为 / Positive Behavior

有助于创造积极环境的行为包括：

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- 使用友好、包容的语言
- 尊重不同的观点和经验
- 优雅地接受建设性批评
- 关注对社区最有利的事情
- 对其他社区成员表示同理心
- 积极参与社区活动
- 帮助新成员融入社区

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Actively participating in community activities
- Helping new members integrate into the community

### 不当行为 / Unacceptable Behavior

不可接受的行为包括：

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- 使用性暗示的语言或图像，以及性关注或性挑逗
- 挑衅、侮辱或贬损性评论，以及人身或政治攻击
- 公开或私下的骚扰
- 未经明确许可，发布他人的私人信息（如地址或电子邮件）
- 在专业环境中被合理认为不当的其他行为

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic address, without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment

## 我们的责任 / Our Responsibilities

项目维护者有责任：

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- 明确可接受行为的标准
- 对任何不可接受行为采取适当、公正的纠正措施
- 删除、编辑或拒绝不符合本行为准则的评论、提交、代码、wiki 编辑、问题和其他贡献
- 在不当行为发生时采取临时或永久性的禁入措施

- Clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior
- Taking appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior
- Deleting, editing, or rejecting comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct
- Banning temporarily or permanently any contributor for behaviors that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful

## 适用范围 / Scope

本行为准则适用于：

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- 项目空间（代码仓库、文档等）
- 公共空间（当个人代表项目或其社区时）
- 与项目相关的其他活动

- Project spaces (code repositories, documentation, etc.)
- Public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community
- Other activities related to the project

## 执行 / Enforcement

### 报告不当行为 / Reporting Unacceptable Behavior

如果你遇到不当行为，请通过以下方式报告：

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it through:

1. **邮件报告** / Email Report
   - 发送邮件至：conduct@liangzhengtao.com
   - Send email to: conduct@liangzhengtao.com

2. **匿名报告** / Anonymous Report
   - 通过 GitHub Issues 报告（可选择匿名）
   - Report through GitHub Issues (can be anonymous)

### 处理流程 / Handling Process

1. **接收报告** / Receive Report
   - 确认收到报告
   - Acknowledge receipt of report

2. **调查** / Investigation
   - 收集相关信息
   - Gather relevant information

3. **决定** / Decision
   - 根据调查结果做出决定
   - Make decision based on investigation results

4. **执行** / Execution
   - 执行决定
   - Execute the decision

5. **通知** / Notification
   - 通知相关人员
   - Notify relevant parties

### 处罚措施 / Consequences

根据违规行为的严重程度，可能采取以下措施：

Depending on the severity of the violation, the following measures may be taken:

1. **警告** / Warning
   - 私下警告
   - Private warning

2. **暂时禁入** / Temporary Ban
   - 暂时禁止参与项目
   - Temporary ban from project participation

3. **永久禁入** / Permanent Ban
   - 永久禁止参与项目
   - Permanent ban from project participation

## 调解 / Mediation

如果需要，我们可以安排调解：

If needed, we can arrange mediation:

- 由中立的第三方进行调解
- 调解过程保密
- 调解结果具有约束力

- Mediation by a neutral third party
- Mediation process is confidential
- Mediation results are binding

## 修改 / Amendments

本行为准则可以修改：

This Code of Conduct may be amended:

- 通过项目维护者的提议
- 通过社区讨论
- 在重大变更时需要通知社区

- Through proposal by project maintainers
- Through community discussion
- Notification to community required for major changes
